Good to know

A few things that apply to offices like yours

Law firms

The North Carolina State Bar's 2024 Formal Ethics Opinion 1 says lawyers can use AI, but protecting client confidences and checking the output is still on the lawyer. I set up the tools so that's easier to do. I'm not a lawyer, so the ethics calls stay with you.

Tax & CPA offices

If you prepare tax returns, federal rules require a written information security plan. The IRS walks through it in Publication 5708. I'll help you write one that matches how your office really works, then make sure the tech backs it up.

Every office

Microsoft ended support for Windows 10 on October 14, 2025. If a computer in the office still runs it, that's usually near the top of the list.
